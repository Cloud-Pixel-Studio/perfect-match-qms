# Product Vision

Perfect Match Digital QMS is a proprietary platform that helps organizations implement, operate, monitor, and improve management systems through Perfect Match methodologies.

Initial and future standard packs may include ISO 9001, IATF 16949, ISO 14001, ISO 45001, AS9120, CMMC Level 1, CMMC Level 2, and future management system frameworks.

The product is not a digital copy of external standards. It stores Perfect Match proprietary controls, activities, workflows, templates, and evidence expectations. External standards are represented only through references and mappings.
