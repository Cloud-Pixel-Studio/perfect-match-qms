# M31.4-A Product Identity / White-Label

Base: `d7f25fd334b4900ba2783fba0c585555582ba991`

This workstream keeps the customer-facing application identity as Perfect
Match QMS without changing Odoo core, routing, access control, record rules,
licensing, API-key authority, device visibility, or proxy behavior.

## Supported extension points

- `web.layout` inheritance supplies the PMQMS favicon and login identity.
- The Odoo `webmanifest` controller is extended to provide PMQMS name,
  short name, theme colors, and the existing PMQMS SVG icon.
- The registered Odoo `title` service is replaced through the registry to
  sanitize technical model/NewId titles while preserving native navigation.

Native Odoo routing such as `/odoo/action-*` remains intentionally unchanged.

## Attribution and scope

The native `Powered by Odoo` attribution is left unchanged pending Product
Owner/legal review. Notification wording, API keys, devices, proxy headers,
and native accessibility findings are outside M31.4-A.

## Verification

Focused source and browser checks must verify the PMQMS title, favicon,
manifest identity, customer navigation, and absence of technical title
values. No product data or customer environment is modified by this change.
