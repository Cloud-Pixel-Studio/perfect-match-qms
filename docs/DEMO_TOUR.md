# Perfect Match QMS Demo Tour

This tour is a short, customer-facing path through the canonical Apex Precision Systems, Inc. Demo. It is intended for product review and release validation, not as a substitute for role-specific access testing.

## Customer Journey

1. **Dashboard**: start with the operating picture and the next actions requiring attention.
2. **Action Center**: open a recommended action and follow it to its source record.
3. **Implementation**: open `Apex Precision ISO 9001 Initial Implementation` and review the 13-phase structure.
4. **Readiness Center**: compare ready, not-started, in-progress, overdue, missing-evidence, under-review, rejected, expired, final-review, and not-applicable examples.
5. **Guided activity**: open one activity and review its objective, guidance, expected output, evidence expectations, and success criteria.
6. **Control**: open the related control instance and inspect status, ownership, evidence, and process context.
7. **Evidence**: review an accepted evidence item, then inspect examples that require review or follow-up.
8. **Risks & Opportunities**: inspect a risk and follow its related improvement action.
9. **Documents**: open a controlled document and review its revision and acknowledgment context.
10. **People & Competency**: review a person, training record, and qualification record.
11. **Performance and Cost of Quality**: review a KPI measurement and a confirmed cost event.
12. **Audit, Customer Quality, Supplier Quality, and Management Review**: inspect representative records that show the wider QMS operating context.

## Scenario Boundaries

The Demo uses fictional Apex Precision Systems data. The implementation project is a source-derived, customer-facing scenario built from the native ISO 9001 framework pack. Historical source records, users, chatter, prompts, and raw imported project tasks are not part of the Demo dataset.

The scenario is deliberately a mixed-maturity view. It demonstrates workflow states and evidence follow-up without claiming that the fictional organization has completed a certification audit.

## Validation Notes

- Use a normal QMS customer persona for the customer journey.
- Use a technical administrator only for technical-maintenance checks.
- Confirm that repeated Demo updates preserve stable records and do not create duplicate projects, controls, activities, requirements, or evidence examples.
- Keep release and customer credentials outside this document.
