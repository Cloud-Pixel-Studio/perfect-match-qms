# ISO 9001:2026 Gap Assessment Workflow

## Purpose

The gap assessment workflow converts an approved implementation or transition scenario into a controlled, company-scoped assessment. It supports first implementations, 2015-to-2026 transitions, recertification, legacy migration, scope expansion, multi-site rollout, integrated systems, and partial implementations.

The workflow contains only Perfect Match-authored operational guidance. It does not reproduce ISO publication text and does not make a certification decision.

## Controlled focus areas

Each assessment initializes the same product-authored focus areas:

1. transition scope and governance;
2. context and stakeholder assumptions;
3. leadership, quality culture, and ethical conduct;
4. risks and opportunities;
5. controlled organizational change;
6. evidence migration and historical integrity.

These areas are operational prompts, not substitutes for an authorized copy of ISO 9001:2026 or competent review.

## Workflow

1. A QMS Manager or Administrator opens an approved transition scenario.
2. **Start Gap Assessment** creates a company-scoped assessment and snapshots its source and target editions.
3. Each area is classified as conforming, partially addressed, gap, or not applicable.
4. Partial and gap areas require a description, action plan, owner, and target date.
5. Not-applicable areas require a documented rationale.
6. Completion records the reviewer and timestamp and makes the assessment immutable.

Readiness is transparent: conforming areas count as 100%, partially addressed areas as 50%, and gaps as 0%. Not-applicable areas are excluded from the denominator.

## Data protection

- Completed assessments and their lines cannot be edited, copied, or deleted.
- Source ISO 9001:2015 profiles, implementation records, evidence, audits, and assessments are not rewritten.
- Global record rules restrict assessments and lines to the user's allowed companies.
- The workflow does not migrate customer records automatically.
- Demo2 and customer environments are updated only through separately approved deployment procedures.

## Next boundary

A later phase may create controlled transition projects and remediation activities from approved assessment gaps. That phase must preserve assessment snapshots and must not infer conformity automatically.
