# PMQMS-023 - Design Risk Model

Priority: HIGH
Project: PMQMS CORE
Module: Risk Management
Cycle: SPRINT 03 - Controls and Evidence
Labels: odoo, architecture, compliance
Dependencies: PMQMS-015

## Objective

Design risk registers, scoring, treatments, owners, reviews, and relationships to controls/processes.

## Description

Deliver a verifiable engineering outcome for `Design Risk Model` while preserving the Odoo-first architecture, least-privilege security model, auditability, and the separation between Perfect Match proprietary controls and external standard references.

## Acceptance Criteria

- The Risk Management decision or design artifact exists in the repository and names open questions explicitly.
- Affected Odoo addons, data models, integrations, and deployment components are identified.
- Dependencies and sequencing constraints are clear enough for implementation planning.
- Security and IP constraints are reviewed before completion.
- Documentation is updated when architecture, behavior, or operating procedure changes.
- Verification evidence is recorded in the work item before closing.
- No copyrighted external standard text is added to seed/demo/configuration data.
